Passwords & Security

Your accounts and devices can contain messages, photos, documents, payment information and other personal data.

Good security makes it much harder for somebody else to access that information.

🔑

Passwords

A password is one of the main barriers protecting an account.

Passwords should be difficult for another person to guess, while still being practical for you to use.

Good password habits

  • Use long passwords.
  • Avoid names and birthdays.
  • Avoid predictable keyboard patterns.
  • Use different passwords for important accounts.
  • Never share your password through messages.
  • Change exposed passwords.

Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds another security check after your password.

This may involve:

  • An authenticator app.
  • A security key.
  • A temporary login code.
  • An approval notification.

Never give another person a verification code used to sign into your account.

🦠

Malware

Malware is software designed to perform harmful or unwanted actions.

Malware can sometimes steal information, display unwanted advertisements, monitor activity or damage files.

Reduce the risk

  • Download software from trusted sources.
  • Avoid suspicious email attachments.
  • Be careful with unknown game cheats.
  • Do not ignore browser security warnings.
  • Keep software updated.
⬆️

Software Updates

Updates are not only for adding new features.

They can also fix security weaknesses discovered after software has been released.

Keeping your operating system, browser and apps updated reduces the chance that known security problems can be used against your device.

💻

Protect Your Devices

Phones, tablets and computers often contain access to many of your online accounts.

  • Use a secure device password or PIN.
  • Enable automatic locking.
  • Install security updates.
  • Keep important files backed up.
  • Do not leave unlocked devices unattended.
  • Use device tracking features when appropriate.
🚨

Signs an Account May Be Compromised

  • Passwords suddenly stop working.
  • Messages appear that you did not send.
  • Unknown devices appear in your account.
  • Your recovery information changes.
  • You receive unexpected login notifications.
  • Settings change without you doing it.

If Your Account Is Hacked

  1. Change your password.
  2. Sign out of unfamiliar devices.
  3. Enable two-factor authentication.
  4. Check your recovery email and phone number.
  5. Remove unfamiliar connected apps.
  6. Check for messages or posts you did not create.
  7. Ask for help if you cannot regain control.
