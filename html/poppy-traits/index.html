Poppy’s Traits

The qualities that define how she moves through the world.





↓Scroll Down↓

👀

Observant

Poppy studies unfamiliar rooms, sounds, and people before deciding how close she wants to be.

😴

Composed

She is rarely hurried. Poppy gives herself time to assess a situation, then acts with quiet certainty.

🧭

Independent

She enjoys company without requiring constant attention and chooses her own place within the room.

🧠

Intelligent

Patterns are learned quickly, particularly those connected with routes, household sequences, and familiar voices.

👀

Sensitive

Changes in tone, pace, and atmosphere are noticed even when nothing has been said directly.

❤️

Loyal

Her attachment is steady rather than dramatic. She checks where people are and settles once everyone is accounted for.

🐕 Poppy at a Glance

A simple comparison of the qualities that stand out most clearly in daily life.
