Breed Profile

Toy Poodle

The toy poodle is the smallest poodle size variety: compact in scale, alert, athletic, highly responsive, and closely attentive to people.

Chapter 1

Scale and Structure

Toy poodles are compact companion dogs. Their smaller size does not reduce their need for movement, learning, or meaningful engagement.

They retain the balanced, athletic structure associated with the poodle breed, expressed at a smaller scale.

Chapter 2

Intelligence

Poodles are known for rapid learning, sensitivity to patterns, and close attention to human behaviour.

Training works best when it combines clarity, variety, and enough mental challenge to hold that attention.

Chapter 3

Coat and Grooming

The dense curly coat grows continuously and requires regular brushing, bathing, and professional trimming.

Poppy’s chocolate coat is kept longer across the crown, where the curls fall unevenly and move naturally rather than forming a perfectly rounded silhouette.

Chapter 4

Activity

Walks, exploration, training, and scent work provide both physical movement and mental stimulation.

The aim is not simply distance. A toy poodle benefits from opportunities to investigate, solve, remember, and respond.

Chapter 5

Temperament

Individual character varies, but the breed is often alert, people-focused, sensitive, and responsive.

Poppy expresses those qualities through close observation, a strong memory for routine, and steady attachment to her household.

Poppy and the Breed

Poppy is one individual, not a checklist. Her breed helps explain the quick learning, close attention, and distinctive coat, while her mature character remains entirely her own.
