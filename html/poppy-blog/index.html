19 July 2026Observation · 3 min

What Poppy hears before we do

Her attention often changes before there is any obvious reason for it. The head lifts first, then turns toward a sound still too distant for anyone else in the room. Most are dismissed quickly. A few deserve continued study.

11 July 2026Walking · 3 min

The difference between distance and progress

A walk with Poppy is not measured well in kilometres. Progress might mean examining one section of grass for several minutes or stopping to understand why a gate is open when it is usually closed.

Poppy rarely changes herself for a camera. The strongest photographs arrive after the device stops being interesting and she returns to watching the room, resting, or looking beyond the frame.

The character of an untidy curl

Poppy’s coat does not remain arranged for long. The longer hair across her crown shifts through the day, gathering into uneven curls that make each photograph recognisably hers.
