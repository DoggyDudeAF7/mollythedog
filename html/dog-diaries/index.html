Molly’s notebook

Naps, towels, and careful decisions

Entry 01 · Laundry Department

The towel acquisition

Today I found a towel inside the laundry basket. The humans had placed it there as though it did not belong to me. I corrected this mistake quietly and carried it somewhere more comfortable.

The operation was nearly perfect. Unfortunately, someone noticed the towel moving by itself.

— Molly

Entry 02 · Weather Review

Rain has been rejected

The sky put water everywhere without asking. I inspected the doorway, considered the conditions, and decided the outdoors was closed.

I will review the situation again after a long emergency nap.

— Molly

Shaina’s incident log

Announcements, investigations, and alerts

Entry 01 · Security Report

A sound happened

At 10:14, I heard something. I barked immediately. At 10:15, somebody said “bark,” so I barked again to confirm that I understood the assignment.

Molly remained asleep. Her commitment to ignoring important events is extraordinary.

— Shaina

Entry 02 · Fruit Investigation

The orange-peel case

An orange peel appeared. I cannot explain why this matters, but it does. I observed it carefully and opened a full investigation.

The humans laughed. They clearly do not understand the seriousness of citrus.

— Shaina

Poppy’s observations

Familiar rooms and thoughtful inspection

Entry 01 · Morning Notes

Everything in its place

The room looked the same as yesterday, which is excellent. I checked the familiar corners, watched everyone begin their routines, and selected a place with a good view.

There is no need to rush when careful observation works perfectly well.

— Poppy

Entry 02 · Grooming Bulletin

The brush returns

The brush appeared again today. Apparently curls require regular administration. I allowed the process while maintaining the expression of someone reviewing a complicated contract.

My coat is tidy. My opinion remains independent.

— Poppy
