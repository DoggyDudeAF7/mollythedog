Poppy

Observant. Independent. Four years old. Entirely at home in Melbourne.





↓Scroll Down↓

🪟

Attentive by Nature

Poppy notices changes in sound, movement, and routine. She prefers to understand a room before settling into it.

🛏️

Independent in Company

She values proximity without spectacle—close enough to observe, comfortable enough to remain entirely herself.

🌙

A Measured Routine

Most days are structured by familiar rooms, regular walks, and long periods of deliberate rest.

🍪

Outside, at Her Own Pace

New ground is approached carefully. Poppy reads a space before moving through it with confidence.

👀

Precise Attention

Small changes are rarely missed. A different sound, an open gate, or an unfamiliar object receives careful study.

🚪

A Memory for Place

Routes, doorways, resting places, and familiar turns are remembered. New spaces become subjects for quiet assessment.

😴

Rest as Routine

Long periods of sleep are not interruptions to the day. They are part of its structure and are taken seriously.

🌧️

Sensitive to Atmosphere

Weather changes the pace of a walk. Wind carries information; rain calls for a more considered route.

🧠

Quiet Intelligence

Poppy learns patterns quickly and responds to small cues in voice, posture, timing, and place.
