Meet The Creator

Hi, I’m Asher.

I’m a 12-year-old student, coder, musician, LEGO builder, and the person behind Molly & Shaina. What began as my dad’s suggestion to make a website about Molly has grown into dog profiles, galleries, comics, breed guides, quizzes, and an AI assistant named Geoff.

🎹

Coder And Musician

I play piano and tenor saxophone, and I love coding. Both music and websites begin with small pieces that gradually turn into something much bigger.

💻

Things I Like Making

How It Grew

From one dog page to a whole tiny universe

01

The suggestion

My dad suggested making a website about Molly, giving me a project where I could practise coding with a subject I already cared about.

02

The dogs arrived

Molly became the sleepy centre of the site, while Shaina added alerts, energy, and a completely different personality. Poppy later received her own corner too.

03

The site expanded

Profiles grew into galleries, habits, minds, FAQs, dog-breed tools, quizzes, a blog, achievements, downloads, and full comic adventures.

04

Geoff joined

Geoff became the site assistant, connecting the pages so visitors could ask questions and explore the growing collection conversationally.
