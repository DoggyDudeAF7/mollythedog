Poppy FAQ

Essential information about Poppy, her routine, and her breed.

❓

How old is Poppy?

Poppy is four years old. She is an adult dog with a settled character and established routines.

🛏️

What breed is Poppy?

She is a chocolate brown toy poodle, the smallest poodle size variety.

🧸

What is her temperament?

Poppy is observant, independent, sensitive to atmosphere, and closely attached to her household.

🍪

How much exercise does she need?

Regular walks, exploration, and mental work support both her physical condition and her attention.

🚪

Why does she choose places near doorways?

Those positions provide a clear view of movement between rooms while allowing her to remain settled.

⚠️

Is Poppy cautious?

She approaches unfamiliar situations carefully. Once she understands a place or person, her confidence becomes clear.

🌧️

What care does her coat require?

Her continuously growing curls require regular brushing, bathing, and professional trimming.

💭

Where does Poppy live?

Poppy lives in Melbourne, where her days are divided between familiar rooms, regular walks, and quiet observation.
