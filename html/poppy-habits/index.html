Morning Survey

🛏️

Deliberate Rest

She returns to familiar resting places with a clear view of the doorway and enough room to remain undisturbed.

Sound Assessment

Evening Check

Before settling for the night, Poppy checks where everyone is and chooses a nearby place to rest.
