Staying Safe Online

The internet is useful for learning, gaming, communicating and creating, but not everything online is trustworthy.


Staying safe means thinking carefully about what you click, what you share and who you communicate with.

🎣

Scams & Phishing

Phishing is when somebody pretends to be a trusted person or company to trick you into giving them information.


A fake message may ask for your password, money, verification code or other personal information.


Warning signs include urgent messages, strange links, unexpected prizes and requests for information that a real company should not need.

🔗

Think Before You Click

Links can lead somewhere different from where they appear to go.


Fake websites can copy the logos, colours and login screens of real services.


Before entering a password or personal information, check the website address carefully.

👁️

Protect Your Privacy

Personal information can reveal much more about you than you expect.


Avoid publicly sharing your home address, phone number, passwords, exact location or other private information.


Always check who can see your posts and review your privacy settings regularly.

📍

Location Sharing

Phones, photos and apps can sometimes reveal where you are.


Only allow location access when an app actually needs it.


Be careful when sharing live locations or posting photos that reveal where you live, study or regularly spend time.

💬

Social Media

Information posted online can spread quickly.


Even if you delete a post later, another person may already have saved, downloaded or screenshotted it.


Think about who can see something before posting it and whether you would still be comfortable with it being seen later.

🎮

Online Gaming

Online games can connect you with people from around the world.


Do not reveal private information in game chats and never give another player your password.


Be suspicious of free currency, suspicious downloads and people asking you to move conversations onto another app.

👣

Your Digital Footprint

Your digital footprint is the trail of information created by your online activity.


Posts, comments, photos, videos and accounts can remain online for a long time.


Before sharing something, consider whether you would still be happy for other people to see it in the future.
