# Molly, Shaina, and Poppy image reference

Use the real dog photographs in `html/images/` as identity references for any new artwork or generated photography.

## Collars

- Molly wears a red collar.
- Shaina wears a light blue and purple collar.
- Poppy wears a white collar decorated with red poppies.

## Appearance and composition

- Keep their real, naturally uneven coat texture. Do not turn their hair into perfectly rounded, sculpted shapes.
- Molly has light cream shaggy hair and tan ears.
- Shaina is smaller, mostly white, with subtle tan around her ears.
- Poppy is a dark chocolate-brown toy poodle with dense, irregular curls and visible flyaways.
- Vary poses, head direction, camera angle, action, and distance between images.
- Avoid repeatedly arranging the dogs in a front-facing seated row.
- Phone wallpapers must be composed or cropped for portrait display. Never place a landscape image over a blurred copy of itself.
